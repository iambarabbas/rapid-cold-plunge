Before You Join

Common questions.

What exactly is a founding membership?

Founding members lock in the $249/month rate permanently as long as their membership remains active. Cancelling forfeits founding status — if you rejoin later, it'll be at the then-current public rate ($299 or higher).

When do you open?

We're currently in our founders-only soft launch phase. The official opening date will be announced to founding members first — well before any public announcement.

Is it truly month-to-month?

Yes. No annual commitment, no minimum contract. Cancel anytime. Just understand that cancelling permanently ends your founding rate.

What's in the welcome box?

Details are revealed closer to opening. Expect something thoughtfully curated and worth the wait. This isn't a branded tote bag situation.

What is Aufguss?

A guided sauna ceremony where a trained Aufguss master pours essential-oil-infused water onto hot stones and fans the steam in choreographed waves. Founding members get first access. Read more on our Sauna page.
