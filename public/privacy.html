Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

RAPID ("we," "our," or "us") operates rapidcoldplunge.com and provides Finnish sauna, cold plunge, and wellness club services at 2990 Midway Drive, Suite A, San Diego, CA 92110. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or become a member.

By using our website or purchasing a membership, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Account credentials for the member portal at join.rapidcoldplunge.com
  • Payment and billing information (processed securely by our payment processor — we do not store your full card number)
  • Health and wellness information you voluntarily provide on intake forms or liability waivers
  • Messages, questions, or other communications you send us

Information Collected Automatically

  • Pages visited, time on site, clicks, and referring URLs
  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Data collected via cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 4)

Information from Third Parties

  • Membership and billing data from our membership management platform
  • Interactions when you engage with our social media accounts on Instagram or Facebook

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process membership applications and manage your account
  • Charge and collect membership fees
  • Communicate with you about your membership, upcoming events, and club updates
  • Send transactional communications such as receipts and billing notices
  • Provide safety guidance and manage health screenings or waivers
  • Improve our website, services, and member experience
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Health Information

To participate in sauna and cold plunge activities, we may ask you to complete a health screening questionnaire or sign a liability waiver. Any health information you provide is used solely to protect your safety and facilitate appropriate facility use. We treat health data with strict confidentiality and do not share it with third parties except as required by law or in a medical emergency.

Nothing on our website or communicated by our staff constitutes medical advice. All wellness information is provided for educational purposes only.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC) collects usage data including pages viewed, session duration, and traffic sources. This data is aggregated and used to improve our website. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the browser add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

You may also configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some website features may not function as intended.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers to operate our business. These providers may have access to your personal information only as necessary to perform their services and are contractually bound to protect it.

  • Payment processing: We use a PCI-compliant payment processor to handle billing. We do not store or have access to your full payment card details.
  • Membership platform: join.rapidcoldplunge.com manages member accounts, billing cycles, and access.
  • Email communications: We may use an email service provider to send newsletters, event announcements, and transactional messages.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (see Section 4).

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your membership is active and for a reasonable period thereafter as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, process refunds, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us (see Section 10). Certain information may be retained longer if required by law.

7. Your Privacy Rights — California Residents

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: You may request a copy of the personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months, including the categories of information, the sources from which we collected it, and how we use it.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

To exercise your rights, email us at info@rapidcoldplunge.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 45 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended exclusively for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

9. Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include secure data transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and working only with reputable third-party processors. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. We will notify active members of material changes by email and will update the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website or membership after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
