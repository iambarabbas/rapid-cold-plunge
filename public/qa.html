The Experience
What is Aufguss?

Aufguss (German for "infusion") is a guided sauna ceremony where a trained Aufguss master pours water infused with essential oils — eucalyptus, birch, pine, citrus — onto the hot sauna stones. Steam erupts instantly. The master then fans the steam in choreographed waves across everyone in the room. It's part theater, part heat therapy, part collective experience.

Founding members get first access to Aufguss sessions at RAPID. Read the full story on our Sauna page.

How long is a cold plunge session?

Most experienced members aim for 2–5 minutes per session. Your first time, even 30 seconds is a real win — the cold shock response is intense and takes time to learn to move through. Our guides will help you build up session by session. The goal isn't duration for its own sake — it's getting the most out of each visit.

What should I wear?

A swimsuit is all you need. We'll have towels available. Bring whatever you like to freshen up after — most members shower and change before heading to work or going about their day. We'll have what you need on site.

Do I need experience with cold plunges or saunas?

No experience necessary. First-timers are not just welcome — they're expected. Our guides are specifically trained to walk new members through the experience before their first session. We cover breathing techniques, what to expect from the cold shock response, and how to approach your first sauna round. By the end of session one, you'll know exactly what you're doing.

Can I do both the cold plunge and sauna in one visit?

Yes — and we encourage it. The contrast protocol (heat, then cold, then recovery) amplifies the benefits of both therapies. Most members cycle 2–3 rounds. A full contrast session typically runs 45–90 minutes depending on how many rounds you do. Your founding membership covers unlimited visits and unlimited time in facility.

The Facility
How cold is the cold plunge?

We maintain our plunges at 50–58°F. That's not approximate — we use industrial-grade chillers with continuous monitoring to hold temperature precisely. You'll never arrive to find a lukewarm plunge. Consistent cold is the whole point.

How hot is the sauna?

Our Finnish sauna runs at 180–195°F. Traditional dry heat — low humidity, high temperature, volcanic rock kiuas. If you're used to steam rooms or infrared saunas, this is a different experience entirely. It's the real thing.

How clean is the water in the cold plunge?

Extremely clean. We run clinical-grade filtration systems with daily sanitation protocols. The water quality is tested regularly and treated like the therapy tool it is. You shouldn't have to think about cleanliness when you're here — so we made sure you won't.

Can I come every day?

Founding memberships include unlimited visits — come as often as you'd like. For cold plunge specifically, research suggests 3–5 sessions per week delivers optimal results, but daily sessions are safe for healthy adults. Our guides can help you dial in the right frequency for your goals.

Are there any health restrictions?

Cold plunge and sauna are safe for most healthy adults. However, we recommend consulting your doctor before starting if you have any cardiovascular conditions, are pregnant, or have other relevant health concerns. Our staff are not medical professionals — they're recovery guides. Your health is your responsibility, and we take safety seriously.
