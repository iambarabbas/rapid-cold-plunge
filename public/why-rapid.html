The founders

Bringing the German “Sauna Paradise” to San Diego.

Max grew up with sauna. James found it when he needed it. Both saw how few places do it right.

Max

Germany → San Diego

Growing up in Germany, I was surrounded by real sauna culture. Later, as a competitive athlete and entrepreneur, I learned that true performance and mental clarity came from disciplined recovery, not just harder training. When I moved to San Diego, I was surprised by the lack of authentic contrast therapy spaces.

James

San Diego native

Growing up in San Diego, saunas weren’t part of my world. I spent years building a career as a rare coin dealer, working long hours and focusing on business success. But that relentless pace took a toll. Weight gain and burnout crept in, a wake-up call that I wasn’t taking care of myself.

Helsinki San Diego

Rooted in Finnish tradition.
Designed for California life.

We created RAPID because we both felt how powerful a simple, consistent sauna and cold plunge practice can be. A calm, modern club where anyone can build it into their week and see results: less stress, faster recovery, clearer thinking.

Supportive, not extreme. Max & James · Founders
