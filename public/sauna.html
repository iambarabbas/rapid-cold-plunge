The heat.

Traditional Finnish sauna and the ancient ritual of Aufguss — guided, intentional, and deeply restorative. This is heat done right.

Finnish Sauna

Dry heat. Deep release.

The Finnish sauna is the oldest form of deliberate heat therapy in the world — and one of the most studied. At RAPID, we run traditional dry heat: a kiuas (sauna stove) loaded with volcanic rock, maintaining temperatures between 180 and 195°F. Low humidity, intense heat, and total surrender.

Inside the sauna, your heart rate elevates to levels comparable to moderate cardio. Core body temperature rises. Blood flow to your skin increases dramatically, driving deep muscle relaxation and activating a cascade of hormonal and cardiovascular benefits. Emerging research links regular sauna use to reduced all-cause mortality and improved cardiovascular health — effects that grow with frequency.

Benefits
🔥 Deep muscle relaxation Elevated heat penetrates muscle tissue, releasing tension and reducing delayed onset soreness more effectively than passive rest.
❤️ Cardiovascular conditioning Heart rate elevates, blood vessels dilate, and cardiac output increases — providing a meaningful cardiovascular stimulus without impact.
🌡️ Detoxification Intense sweating drives elimination of metabolic waste through the skin, clearing what your kidneys and liver can't process as quickly.
😌 Stress reduction Sauna promotes the release of endorphins and dynorphins — natural mood elevators — creating a lasting calm that extends well beyond the session.
🤝 Community ritual Sharing a sauna is one of the oldest social rituals in human culture. At RAPID, we take that seriously — it's part of what makes the membership different.

The Ceremony

The ceremony.

Aufguss (German for "infusion") is a guided sauna ritual that transforms a standard sauna session into something closer to a performance. A trained Aufguss master pours water infused with essential oils — eucalyptus, birch, pine, citrus — onto the hot stones. Steam fills the room instantly.

What follows is choreography. The master uses a large towel to fan the steam in precise, rhythmic waves — directing heat across every person in the room. The waves intensify. Temperature spikes. Sweat pours. And then it lifts. Breath by breath, the ceremony reaches its crescendo and releases.

It's part theater, part heat therapy, part collective experience. Nothing else like it. Founding members at RAPID get first access to Aufguss sessions — an experience most gyms have never heard of, let alone offer.

The Full Protocol

The protocol: heat, then cold.

Pairing sauna with cold plunge isn't just convenient — it's synergistic. The contrast amplifies the benefits of both therapies and creates an experience that can't be replicated by either alone.

🔥
Heat Phase

10–20 minutes in the Finnish sauna. Core temperature rises. Muscles loosen. Blood flow opens to your extremities. Sweat is the signal — you're ready.

❄️
Cold Phase

Into the plunge. Vasoconstriction drives warm blood back to your core. The contrast is intense — and that intensity is where the magic happens.

🔄
Repeat & Recover

Most members cycle 2–3 rounds. The recovery between rounds — just sitting, breathing — is part of the protocol. You leave different than you arrived.
